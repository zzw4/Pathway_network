## Local-support audit

The exploratory propensity model used ER, PR, HER2, the other primary source, ERBB2 amplification and CCND1 amplification. It is a support diagnostic rather than a causal model.

| Source | carriers | overlap ESS carriers | ATE ESS carriers | max ATE weight | OOF propensity AUC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| PIK3CA GOF | 179 | 176.7 | 120.8 | 21.8 | 0.621 |
| TP53 LOF | 93 | 90.1 | 53.5 | 29.3 | 0.719 |

Interpretation:

- Both individual sources provisionally exceed the local-ESS gate of 30.
- PIK3CA has strong overlap under the currently available covariates.
- TP53 remains supportable but has more extreme weights and needs explicit leverage filtering/grouping/abstention.
- The PIK3CA × TP53 interaction cannot be a primary neural-network admission test under the frozen four-cell rule.
- Purity, ploidy, histology, grade, age, technical covariates and broader co-alterations are not yet joined; all results must be repeated on the frozen GDC-harmonized cohort.

## Current decision

**Continue WP0.** The TCGA marginal-support and individual-source overlap gates provisionally pass. The interaction gate fails for the PIK3CA × TP53 pair. The next critical task is CPTAC-BRCA schema, linked sample count, source carriers and source-specific non-RNA intermediate coverage.
