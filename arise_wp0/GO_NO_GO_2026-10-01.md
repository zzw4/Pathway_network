# ARISE cancer-level network: interim go/no-go decision

## Decision

**Superseded on 2 October 2026 for the primary ARISE decision.** The result below is a NO-GO for the pooled WP4155 expression-reconstruction formulation only. It is not a decision on the frozen alteration-removal attribution task; see `SOURCE_SPECIFIC_EXECUTION_CORRECTION_2026-10-02.md`.

UCEC currently has the best evidence for a cancer-specific, multi-module signalling network. It has sufficient TCGA sample support, multiple alteration entry points, and two independently supported alteration-to-intermediate chains. However, an unpruned OmniPath graph is too dense and nonspecific. The next design stage must produce a compact UCEC network analogous to a curated cancer WikiPathway before modelling.

## Cancer-level RNA screen

Using conservative functional alteration classes, top-100 PROGENy footprints, covariate-adjusted robust regression, directional FDR 10%, and at least 20 carriers:

| Cancer | Supported routes | Supported programmes |
|---|---:|---|
| UCEC | 4 | MAPK, PI3K, WNT, p53 |
| BRCA | 3 | EGFR, PI3K, p53 |
| COADREAD | 3 | MAPK, PI3K, p53 |
| LUAD | 2 | MAPK, p53 |

UCEC supported routes are KRAS→MAPK, PIK3R1→PI3K, CTNNB1→WNT and damaging TP53→lower p53 footprint. PIK3R1→PI3K subsequently failed the CPTAC phosphosite gate and is not considered validated.

## UCEC independent intermediate evidence

- **KRAS→MAPK passes.** CPTAC ERK activation module beta 0.45, P=0.015, with MAPK1 T185/Y187 and MAPK3 Y204 in the expected direction after total-protein and covariate adjustment.
- **CTNNB1→WNT passes at total-protein level.** Activating CTNNB1 mutation associates with beta-catenin protein accumulation: beta 0.569, P=2.6e-7, 23 CPTAC carriers. Destruction-site phosphopeptides are too sparse for a site-level conclusion.
- **TP53→p53 is RNA-supported but not independently passed.** Only 11 conservative CPTAC carriers; CDKN1A protein is directionally lower but misses multiplicity correction.
- **PIK3R1/PTEN/PIK3CA→PI3K fails the current phosphosite definition.** This branch must not be presented as validated.

## Graph audit

The unrestricted signed OmniPath graph contains 21,178 directed signed edges over 6,179 single-gene nodes. At six propagation steps, KRAS and CTNNB1 each reach about 278 transcription factors; even at three steps they reach about 149. This is too dense to preserve source-specific interpretation. The union covers only 19–41% of the selected pathway-responsive targets through high-confidence DoRothEA edges.

Therefore the full database graph fails the interpretability gate. This does not invalidate the biological project; it invalidates the naive graph-construction strategy.

## Required next stage

Construct a complete UCEC signalling atlas anchored on the full current WikiPathways Endometrial cancer pathway WP4155, then augment it from explicitly selected Reactome/SIGNOR evidence:

1. RAS–RAF–MEK–ERK;
2. WNT destruction complex–beta-catenin–TCF/LEF;
3. p53–cell-cycle/apoptosis;
4. PI3K–AKT retained as an unvalidated candidate module;
5. ERBB signalling;
6. adherens junction;
7. FGFR receptor branches and cross-talk edges when signed and supported by curated sources.

The first complete conversion contains 63 unique WP4155 genes plus PIP3. Expansion of WP families/complexes and three explicit ERBB2 bridge pairs yields 109 core edges. The eight terminal TFs are ELK1, FOS, FOXO3, LEF1, MYC, TCF7, TCF7L2 and TP53. Source-specific reachability is retained: KRAS reaches ELK1/FOS; CTNNB1 reaches the TCF/LEF/MYC branch; PI3K/PTEN inputs reach FOXO3; TP53 retains its own programme. In contrast, adding all 126 within-node-set OmniPath candidates makes every alteration source reach all eight TFs and is rejected as the default graph.

The output layer must retain evidence types separately. High-confidence DoRothEA directly covers only 7/100 top MAPK footprint genes from KRAS-reachable TFs, 4/100 WNT genes from CTNNB1-reachable TFs, 27/100 p53 genes from TP53, and 0/100 PI3K genes from FOXO3. PROGENy footprint edges may supplement coverage as module-to-expression observation links, but must not be mislabeled as direct TF regulation.

## Output-layer and linear feasibility gate

The fixed output layer contains 500 PROGENy pathway-response edges across MAPK, WNT, PI3K, EGFR and p53, plus 1,471 unique high-confidence DoRothEA TF-target edges. Evidence types remain separate. There are 65 output genes supported by both types, 416 footprint-only genes and 1,199 direct-TF-only genes. The primary modelling endpoint is restricted to 458 measurable footprint-supported genes; direct-only genes are interpretive secondary outputs.

A five-fold cross-validated multi-output ridge baseline was run on 527 TCGA UCEC samples:

- clinical/subtype covariates only: mean held-out R2 = 0.0362;
- clinical plus functional mutation and CNA inputs: mean held-out R2 = 0.0420;
- mean alteration increment = 0.00579;
- 137/458 genes have delta R2 > 0.01;
- 229/458 genes improve at all.

The increment exceeds all 100 joint alteration/CNA permutations performed within molecular subtype (empirical P=0.0099). Module-level mean delta R2 is 0.0127 for WNT, 0.0105 for MAPK, 0.00436 for PI3K, 0.00421 for EGFR and 0.00015 for p53. Thus there is sufficient non-linear-model input signal to justify a minimal prototype, but the expected absolute predictive gain is modest and concentrated in WNT/MAPK.

## Model-development boundary

The minimal prototype proceeds under these fixed conditions:

- connects at least three alteration entry classes to distinct terminal TF sets;
- retains source-specific reachability rather than converging immediately on the same hubs;
- covers at least half of the empirically supported RNA targets after a sparse TF decoder is attached;
- reproduces the two positive-control chains without using CPTAC outcomes to select edges;
- admits linear diffusion and pathway-score baselines under exactly the same graph and data splits.

The model is stopped if it does not improve held-out performance or CPTAC intermediate alignment over the ridge baseline. Complete inclusion of WP4155 does not imply that every module must be predictive; weak PI3K, ERBB or p53 performance must be reported rather than removed post hoc.

## Minimal neural prototype result

A signed sparse propagation prototype was implemented with 64 signalling entities, 106 usable signed core edges, five module readouts and 543 typed output edges. It was evaluated under the same five folds as the ridge baseline. Clinical out-of-fold ridge predictions were frozen, so the neural network was responsible only for alteration-associated residual expression. A node-permuted graph with identical topology, parameter count and decoder served as the structural control.

After correcting the biological branch to initialise near zero and testing a gated parameterisation:

| Model | Mean held-out R2 | Median held-out R2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Clinical + mutation/CNA ridge | 0.04200 | 0.02765 |
| WP4155 signed propagation | 0.03468 | 0.01761 |
| Randomised graph propagation | 0.03466 | 0.01759 |

The correct graph improves over the random graph by only 0.000024 mean R2 and remains below the ridge model. It also loses to ridge in every module: EGFR 0.0325 versus 0.0382, MAPK 0.0436 versus 0.0555, PI3K 0.0336 versus 0.0395, WNT 0.0274 versus 0.0417, and p53 0.0415 versus 0.0432. Thus even the empirically strongest WNT/MAPK modules do not rescue the constrained model.

This satisfies the prespecified stop condition. There is reproducible alteration-associated RNA signal, but the signal is not preferentially captured by the current WikiPathways propagation structure. Further tuning, adding layers, or introducing context gates would risk converting the project into post-hoc architecture search without evidence that the biological graph is useful.

The graph and audits remain reusable assets, but this specific task should not proceed to full neural-model development. Any rescue must change the scientific task or provide a new independent source of supervision; it should not merely increase model complexity.
